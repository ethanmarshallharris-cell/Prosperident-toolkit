PROSPERIDENT NOTEBOOK TO WORD EXPORTER
Internal use only.

Exports a whole case notebook from OneNote (web) to one Word document:
cover page, table of contents, every section and page in order, pictures,
checkboxes and page numbers. File attachments are saved in a folder beside
the document. Optionally also makes a PDF.

YOU NEED
  - Windows with Microsoft Word installed.
  - Your Prosperident account, with access to the case's notebook.

SETUP (once per computer)
  1. If this came as a download or email attachment: right-click the .zip,
     choose Properties, tick "Unblock", click OK. Then extract it to a folder
     of your choice, e.g. Documents\Notebook Exporter.
  2. Put the case list, casecapture-config.json, in that folder. It is not
     part of the download because it names clients - ask David for it.
  3. Double-click "Sign In For OneNote.bat". It shows a short code and a web
     address. Open the address, enter the code, and sign in with YOUR OWN
     Prosperident account. (The window mentions a "service account" - ignore
     that; it refers to the office automation.)

TO EXPORT A NOTEBOOK
  1. Double-click "Export Case Notebook to Word.bat".
  2. It lists the cases. Type the case name exactly as shown and press Enter.
  3. Answer Y if you also want a PDF.
  4. The document appears in Documents\Prosperident Notebook Exports\.
     A large notebook can take several minutes.

IMPORTANT
  - The exported document contains client and patient information. File it
    in the case document library. Do not email it or keep stray copies.
  - It is a snapshot. The notebook remains the working record.
  - Handwriting (ink), audio and video recordings cannot be exported - the
    OneNote service does not provide them. A page OneNote cannot deliver
    appears with a note in its place.
  - The case list (casecapture-config.json) is a snapshot. When new cases
    are opened, ask David for an updated copy of that one file.
  - UPDATES: after an export the window says when a newer version is on
    www.prosperident.com/tool-suite. Extract the new download over this
    folder; your casecapture-config.json and sign-in are kept.
  - Every run writes a log to the "logs" folder here. If something goes
    wrong, send that log to David.

FILES
  Export Case Notebook to Word.bat   - run this to export
  Sign In For OneNote.bat            - one-time sign-in
  Export-NotebookToWord.ps1          - the exporter
  Get-DelegatedToken.ps1             - OneNote sign-in / credential renewal
  GraphRetry.ps1                     - retries when Microsoft is busy
  _log.cmd                           - writes the logs
  casecapture-config.json            - the case list (from David; not in the download)
